SupplyToy is an independent toy review site. We write for parents, grandparents and anyone else who has to choose a toy for a child aged roughly 0 to 12 and would rather not guess.

## What we actually do

Most toy listings tell you what a toy *is*. We try to answer the questions that decide whether it gets played with: what the toy demands from a child, which stage it suits, how long the interest usually lasts, and where it falls down.

That last part matters more than the rest. A review that cannot say "skip this one" is not a review. Where a category has a genuine safety problem — small parts, magnets, button batteries, water beads, cord loops — we say so plainly, and we would rather talk you out of a purchase than take a commission on it.

## How the site is funded

SupplyToy is reader-supported. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, and buying through links on this page may earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you.

We do not accept payment for placement, and a toy being easy to link to has never been a reason to recommend it. Where we link to a product category, it is because we are recommending that category — not because the link pays.

## Our sources

Safety guidance on this site comes from the bodies that actually publish it: the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), the American Academy of Pediatrics, the CDC, the FDA, and the relevant standards and regulations (ASTM F963, 16 CFR parts 1250 and 1500, the EU Toy Safety Directive, and others). Where we quote a figure, we name the report and the year it came from.

We are not doctors, and nothing here is medical advice. If you are dealing with a suspected ingestion, an injury, or a recall, contact the appropriate authority directly — for recalls, start at cpsc.gov/Recalls or SaferProducts.gov, or call 800-638-2772.

## Corrections

Toy lines change, standards get revised, and a recommendation that was right two years ago can be wrong today. If you spot something out of date or wrong, tell us and we will fix it.
